Boundaries of the Niche: Exergy, Institutional Claims, and Biological Continuity in Coupled Human Systems

Research papers and citations

Henrik Hagnell · Draft · 15 November 2026 · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24242901

Abstract

This draft develops a coupled state-space framework for reconstructing the material, institutional and biological conditions of human systems. It separates physical exergy reserves and persistent infrastructure from institutional rules, claims and models, while treating populations as adaptive biological systems with distinct response and developmental timescales. The framework examines how buffer depletion, institutional lag, demographic adjustment and cross-layer feedback affect the accessibility of incumbent and successor configurations. Scenario reconstruction is conditional on observable constraints, rather than a claim of inevitable collapse or a uniquely prescribed successor regime.
