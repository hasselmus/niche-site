About this reference guide

A structural reading guide that locates the seven companion manuscripts around the parent framework. It groups them into historical genesis, micro-dynamical and somatic sensing, cultural and epistemic mapping, and antagonistic perturbation, with an interlock table and suggested analytical precedence order.

Keywords: reading guide; companion architecture; cross-references; DST–NCT.

Relationship to main framework: Reference map rather than an independent research paper; identifies where each of the seven companions attaches to the parent framework.

Function: Not an empirical hypothesis; this document maps dependencies and recommended reading order.
