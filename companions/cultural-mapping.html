Cultural Simulation, Counterfactual Coordination, and Successor-State Telemetry

Research papers and citations

Henrik Hagnell · 15 November 2026 · Companion collection DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24248701 (shared across seven papers and the overview)

Abstract

This companion examines fictional worlds, games, reenactment and other cultural simulations as possible low-cost environments for exploring counterfactual social and resource arrangements. It distinguishes rehearsal that transfers into physical skills or coordination from symbolic substitution without such transfer. A six-domain observation matrix separates represented institutional rules, threat contingencies, cohort participation, network topology, conversion into action and production or patronage structures. Cultural signals remain subordinate to independently measured physical and demographic conditions.
