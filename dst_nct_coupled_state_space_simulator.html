1. The Four Coupled State Variables x(t) = (b(t), m(t), a(t), u(t))

  • u(t) External Driving Conditions: Basal resource gradients, EROI depletion, and planetary climate forcing that accelerate structural decay D_s(s, u).
  • m(t) Material Niche: Comprises stored usable exergy B(t) and persistent constructed infrastructure s(t). Maintaining s(t) requires continuous physical reinvestment I_s.
  • a(t) Institutional Coordinate System: Encodes rules, models, and forward-dated financial claims a_claims(t) compounding on the fast financial clock (τ_fin).
  • b(t) Biological & Demographic Substrate: Self-calibrating human population distributed across an environmental sensitivity gradient (high-plasticity "Orchids" vs. high-inertia "Dandelions").

2. Structural Cannibalisation (Ch. 2 & 5)

When net exergy surplus P_surplus turns negative while a_claims(t) continues compounding, an institution in Niche Lock preserves its symbolic solvency by liquidating buffers B(t), deferring maintenance I_s, and compressing household care buffers Σ_care. This creates Representational Divergence: symbolic GDP rises while the physical substrate decays.

3. Centrifugal & Centripetal Decoupling (Ch. 9–12)

As Σ_care erodes, the plastic youth vanguard (Orchids) truncates horizons and exits multi-decade debt/reproductive pipelines (Centrifugal Decoupling). Meanwhile, adult cohorts (Dandelions) stay compliant via Schema Inertia until the Intergenerational Pincer triggers an abrupt hysteresis snap. Inside a(t), operators shift to In-Situ Patch Foraging, mutating N₀ into a Chimeric Institution.

4. How to Test Successor Viability & Reverse Hysteresis (Ch. 12.4)

Crossing the basin boundary Σ terminates the macro-coordinate system N₀, calving off fast-governed Capture Enclaves (N_i,capture) and low-overhead Subsistence/Cooperative Networks (N_i,exit). By activating Voluntary Claims Jubilee & Modular N_i early enough—or stabilizing local renewable buffers after bifurcation—you can restore T_cover > T_recur across the developmental window (τ_dev) and observe Bi-Directional Orchid Re-Nucleation.
