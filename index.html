Boundaries of the Niche

Exergy, institutional claims, and biological continuity in coupled human systems.

This site will present research notes and computational explorations of how material throughput, constructed environments, institutional representations and population dynamics interact across different timescales.

Framework and notes

Readable chapters, definitions, working hypotheses and revisions will be published here. The source framework is currently a draft.

Interactive models

Planned browser-based models will explore resource buffering, structural maintenance, representational divergence, threshold transitions and the independent viability conditions of incumbent and successor regimes.

Simulations are under development. No model results are published yet.
